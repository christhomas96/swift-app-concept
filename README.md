# Swift family app concept

A clickable concept prototype for a Swift School family app, made to start a conversation with the school.

**This is an unofficial concept.** It is not a Swift School product, and the school has not reviewed or endorsed it. The Swift School name and logo belong to the school and will be removed on request.

- The family, newsletter wording, and alerts are made up.
- Hours, event dates, tuition, and other public facts come from theswiftschool.org, checked on November 3, 2026. They may be out of date.
- Buttons do not send anything or contact anyone.
